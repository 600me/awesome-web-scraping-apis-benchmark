A PRACTICAL BUYING GUIDE

ZenRows vs ScrapingBee. Or a ScraperAPI alternative?

The best scraping API for Cloudflare depends on usable records, target coverage and cost. For a bypass DataDome scraping API workflow, test identical URLs and required fields across trials.

  1. Shortlist ZenRows for difficult anti-bot targets and ScrapingBee for browser interaction.
  2. Compare ScraperAPI for proxy-backed collection and Crawlbase for crawling plus storage.
  3. Budget with plan price ÷ credits × requests × credits/request. At $49 per 250k credits, 10k one-credit requests allocate $1.96; at 25 credits each, they allocate $49.
  4. Check the actual billed credits and usable output before choosing a plan. The displayed 98.5%, 97.8%, 96.5% and 95.2% are baseline inputs.
