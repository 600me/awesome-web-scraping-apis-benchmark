The $0.49/GB Proxy Illusion: How a 200-Line CLI Exposes Real Scraping Costs

A reseller quotes $0.49 per 1,000 requests. At 70% usable yield the real price is $0.70, and a naive three-retry policy pushes it to $1.12. The check-proxy-tax CLI measures status codes, blocked reasons, latency percentiles and effective cost per 1,000 usable responses with no account and no dependencies.

  • $0.49 sticker → $0.70 at 70% yield (1.43×)
  • 2.29× multiplier with three retries
  • 1M usable pages: $490 → $700/month
